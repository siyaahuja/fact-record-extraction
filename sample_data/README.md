# sample_data: demo documents and planted contradictions

Two developments: **Kelso Villa, Upper Bristol Road, Bath BA1 3AU** (Grade II offices, Carter Jonas) and **St Huberts Close, Gerrards Cross** (Strutt & Parker).

- `real/`: the four real brochures (Kelso PDFs are scanned images with no text layer: use OCR/vision).
- `synthetic/`: fictional documents generated by `generate_synthetic.py` (reportlab). Every one has `synthetic: true` in its PDF Subject/Keywords and a small 'Demo document' footer. All people, firms, title and certificate numbers are invented.
- `previews/`: page 1 of each synthetic PDF as PNG.
- `manifest.json`: every document with development, unit, doc_type, issuer, date, authority tier, synthetic flag, page count and sha256.
- `contradictions.json`: the answer key (each contradiction with evidence doc/page/quote, type, expected resolution, voice-agent line) plus fields that agree.

Rebuild: `python3 generate_synthetic.py && python3 build_manifest.py`

- **C03** (unit_or_measurement_mismatch): Never give a single unlabelled area. Once verified: net and gross separately, each with its basis.
- **C04** (true_conflict): Conflicting: don't quote a net area. 'The measured survey and the brochure give different net areas; an agent will confirm.'
- **C05** (true_conflict): Pending: don't quote a gross area until verified.

- **C11** (official_record_mismatch): 'The lower ground floor appears to have been let; an agent will confirm what's still available.'

- **C14** (unit_or_measurement_mismatch): Give the area with its basis; never a single unlabelled figure.
- **C15** (plot_or_type_variation): For No. 59: 'The EPC rating is D.' Never answer with the other house's C.

## Caveats

- Kelso brochure dates (April / October 2026) come from the spec; neither PDF prints a date (`date_source: manual`).
- The No. 59 brochure prints 'Particulars prepared April' without a year in decodable text; 2026 comes from the file metadata (created 6 May 2026). Parts of its text layer are Caesar-shifted by +29 (broken font encoding).
- `parking_allocation_schedule` isn't in the schema's doc_type list yet; it's treated as tier 3 here (the spec names allocation schedules as authoritative for parking). Add it to the enum.
- C04/C05 are 'true conflicts' across tiers (survey vs brochure); the higher tier is the candidate but a human must sign off because area is material.
