# From the product spec (pasted by the team lead)

## Fact catalogue (category: authoritative source; why it drifts)
- Price and incentives: latest signed price list / developer instruction; reservation agreement once under offer. Drift: cuts issued in new brochure not pushed to portal. Evidence: Kelso Villa April £700,000 vs October £600,000; listings still at £700,000.
- Tenure, lease length, ground rent, service charge: current Land Registry title and lease. Drift: marketing shorthand; stale Price Paid tenure. Evidence: Kelso tenure rows; Lumley House listing vs 2016 Price Paid.
- Floor areas and dimensions: measured survey with basis stated (NIA, GIA); EPC floor area official but own basis. Drift: sq m to sq ft conversion errors; NIA stated as GIA. Evidence: Kelso April brochure printed 185 sq m as 2,700 sq ft.
- Specification, finishes, bathrooms: latest signed specification / schedule of finishes, unit or plot level. Drift: brochure versions, show home vs actual, plot-type variation (shower vs bath).
- Energy (EPC, heating, solar): official EPC register (rating + certificate date). Drift: predicted vs as-built; marketing ahead of certificate.
- Parking and EV: lease, title or deed of grant, allocation schedule. Drift: spaces reallocated; "parking available" vs a named space.
- Also in catalogue (later): completion dates, warranties, building safety/EWS1, broadband, covenants, pets/subletting, availability, accessibility, management company.

## Source-authority hierarchy (highest first; recency applies within a tier; newer brochure never beats a contract)
1. Signed legal instrument: title, lease, sale contract, reservation agreement.
2. Current official register extract: Land Registry title, EPC certificate (always with register date). Price Paid tenure is as at sale date.
3. Latest signed specification or measured survey with basis stated.
4. Latest signed price list or formal developer instruction.
5. Latest marketing brochure / particulars (Kelso October 2026 > April 2026, same issuer and type).
6. Portal listing, CRM note, verbal remark.
Human must sign off true conflicts, official-record mismatches, and any override of a higher tier.

## Contradiction types (type: rule; what the voice agent says while unresolved)
- True conflict (same unit, field, basis; can't both be right): no auto-pick by recency among peers; human sign-off; loser kept in history. Agent: do not quote either.
- Version supersession (later doc, same issuer): later is candidate; material fields still confirmed by human. Agent: pending, do not quote.
- Unit or measurement mismatch (Kelso 185 sq m printed as 2,700 sq ft; 185 sq m ≈ 1,990 sq ft NIA, 2,700 sq ft is GIA): normalise and label basis; arithmetic error = document error, not two areas. Agent: never give a single unlabelled area; once verified give net and gross separately.
- Scope difference (development-wide claim vs unit fact): never copy a development claim onto a unit. Keep both at stated scope.
- Plot or type variation: resolve entity first, never merge plots/types.
- Stale listing vs latest document: latest verified document wins; flag listing.
- Official-record mismatch (Lumley House, 56 Elliott Road, Cirencester GL7 1YS: listing freehold, Price Paid leasehold 2016): do not auto-correct; human checks current title.
